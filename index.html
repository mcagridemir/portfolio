01 — About

Senior iOS Engineer at OBSS & BKT — building the banking apps that hundreds of thousands of customers in Kosovo and Albania rely on every day.

"Six years in, I care about code that ships, teams that communicate, and problems worth solving."

Over six-plus years I've shipped production apps for global brands — Nike, Under Armour, Bloomingdale's — and enterprise banking clients, leading architecture decisions and CI/CD pipelines in multinational teams working across Europe, the UK, Dubai, and Albania.

Alongside iOS, my work has naturally taken me toward machine learning and data. At carbon.ninja I built automated ETL pipelines for energy and weather data and trained predictive models for electricity consumption forecasting. On the side I've explored deep learning — building a pneumonia detection model from chest X-rays using EfficientNet-B0 with Grad-CAM — and brought on-device inference into iOS via Core ML. It's a direction I enjoy and want to keep building in.

My Turkish Software Engineering degree has been officially evaluated by the KMK Zentralstelle für ausländisches Bildungswesen as equivalent to a German Bachelor's. I hold a telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule certificate (March 2026) and IELTS Academic 6.5 (April 2026).
